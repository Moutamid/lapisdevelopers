Lapis Awards

Lapis Broker Awards 2022 celebrated the professionals driving Dubai’s real estate momentum.

By Lapis Developments
May 2022
Award Ceremony

In 2022, Lapis Properties hosted its annual broker recognition event to honour the sales professionals who delivered exceptional results across Dubai’s residential, commercial, and investment property sectors.

The winners were chosen for their consistency, client trust, deep market knowledge, and ability to secure transactions in a competitive and fast-moving market. The evening celebrated the brokers who shaped strong portfolio growth and elevated the customer experience.

“The 2022 awards recognised the brokers who transformed ambition into action, helping buyers, investors, and developers move confidently through one of Dubai’s most dynamic property cycles.”

Lapis Properties Leadership

2022 BROKER AWARDS

Top winners and standout categories

Winners at the 2022 ceremony included the top-performing residential sales brokers, leading commercial advisors, and specialists in off-plan and investment property transactions. Each award reflected sustained results, strong negotiation skills, and a clear commitment to service excellence.

From luxury home sales to high-value investment deals, the recognised brokers demonstrated the standards that continue to define Lapis Properties as a trusted name in Dubai real estate.

Highlights from the event

The 2022 Lapis Awards brought together developers, brokers, and industry partners for an evening centred on performance, recognition, and long-term partnership. It marked a year of strong market demand, rising investor confidence, and a growing appetite for premium property opportunities across Dubai.

In addition to celebrating individual excellence, the event reinforced Lapis Properties’ commitment to building a motivated, knowledgeable, and client-first brokerage culture.
