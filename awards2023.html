Lapis Awards

Lapis Broker Awards 2023 recognised leadership in a fast-growing Dubai market.

By Lapis Developments
April 2023
Award Ceremony

During the 2023 Lapis Awards, the developer celebrated the brokers who delivered standout performance in a market defined by rising demand, new launches, and investor confidence across Dubai.

The winners reflected the strength of the city’s property ecosystem, from luxury residential sales and off-plan launches to commercial transactions and repeat-client relationships. Recognised brokers stood out for market intelligence, negotiation skill, and a commitment to transparent advisory.

“In 2023, our awards celebrated a generation of brokers who combined expertise with trust, helping clients secure the right opportunities in one of the world’s most competitive real estate markets.”

Lapis Properties Leadership

2023 BROKER AWARDS

Recognised excellence across the market

Top winners in 2023 included leading residential brokers, investment specialists, and commercial advisors who consistently delivered premium outcomes for buyers and sellers alike. The event also recognised teams that supported high-volume transactions and strengthened the Lapis brand across key communities and business hubs.

The 2023 ceremony was a reminder that broker excellence remains central to growth, confidence, and customer satisfaction in Dubai’s property sector.

Why the awards mattered

Beyond recognition, the 2023 awards were designed to reinforce standards across the brokerage network. Lapis Properties continues to reward performance that puts client value, market understanding, and professionalism at the centre of every transaction.

The awards also highlighted the importance of relationship-building, long-term trust, and disciplined execution in a market shaped by rapid change.
