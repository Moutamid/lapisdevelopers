Lapis Awards

Lapis Broker Awards 2024 recognised the brokers defining Dubai’s next chapter of growth.

By Lapis Developments
April 2024
Award Ceremony

At the 2024 Lapis Broker Awards, the company honoured the professionals who delivered exceptional sales performance across Dubai’s most sought-after residential and commercial locations.

From luxury apartments and waterfront communities to investment-driven transactions and strategic commercial deals, the winners reflected the diversity and professionalism of Dubai’s property market. Their results underscored both market expertise and a high standard of client care.

“The 2024 awards celebrated the brokers who not only closed transactions but built long-term confidence and trust in a city that continues to attract global buyers and investors.”

Lapis Properties Leadership

2024 BROKER AWARDS

Winners across key categories

The 2024 programme recognised top-performing brokers in residential sales, commercial advisory, off-plan launches, and investor transactions. These winners were selected based on performance, market knowledge, and consistency in delivering value to both clients and developers.

The event also acknowledged the role of relationship-driven brokerage in strengthening Dubai’s position as a global real estate destination.

Driving standards in Dubai property

The annual awards reinforce Lapis Properties’ focus on quality, accountability, and performance. By creating a platform to celebrate broker excellence, the company continues to support a stronger, more transparent, and more client-focused real estate ecosystem.

It was a year defined by confidence, expansion, and premium demand—and the winners reflected the very best of what the Dubai market has to offer.
