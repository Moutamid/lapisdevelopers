Lapis Broker Awards 2025 recognised the Dubai brokers setting new standards in service and sales.

By Lapis Developments
May 2025
Award Ceremony

The 2025 Lapis Awards celebrated brokers who led sales activity, delivered premium client experiences, and strengthened buyer confidence in Dubai’s resilient property market.

Winners represented some of the city’s most effective sales professionals across villa communities, apartment towers, commercial assets, and high-growth investment corridors. The recognition reflected not only transaction numbers, but also trust, consistency, and strategic understanding of client goals.

“The 2025 ceremony recognised the teams and individuals who continue to raise the benchmark for professionalism, market knowledge, and customer care in Dubai real estate.”

Lapis Properties Leadership

2025 BROKER AWARDS

A year of excellence and momentum

Top winners in 2025 were recognised in categories that included residential sales leadership, investment guidance, commercial advisory, and community-focused service. These awards reflected the growing sophistication of Dubai’s real estate market and the role of strong brokerage in converting demand into successful deals.

The event also reinforced the importance of a client-first approach in a market driven by lifestyle appeal, capital growth, and evolving buyer expectations.

Why it matters

As Dubai continues to attract global investors and end-users, broker performance remains central to successful outcomes. The 2025 awards highlighted the professionals delivering value through expertise, responsiveness, and a deep understanding of the market.

For Lapis Properties, recognising these brokers is part of a broader commitment to building a highly trusted, high-performing, and future-ready real estate network.
