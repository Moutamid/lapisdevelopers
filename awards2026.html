Lapis Broker Awards 2026 recognised the professionals leading Dubai’s next era of property growth.

By Lapis Developments
April 2026
Award Ceremony

The 2026 Lapis Awards celebrated the brokers who continue to shape Dubai’s momentum in residential, commercial, and investment property. This year’s event recognised professionals who combined market expertise with an unwavering focus on client trust and service quality.

Winning brokers stood out for delivering high-value transactions, understanding buyer behaviour, and guiding clients through both premium developments and strategic investment opportunities. Their achievements reflected the strength and maturity of Dubai’s property market and the role of broker excellence in sustaining it.

“The 2026 awards underline our continued commitment to rewarding performance, professionalism, and dedication across the full spectrum of Dubai real estate.”

Lapis Properties Leadership

2026 BROKER AWARDS

Recognising outstanding achievement

Winners in 2026 included top residential brokers, high-performing commercial advisors, and specialists in new-launch sales and investor advisory. The categories reflected the breadth of service required in a market driven by luxury demand, institutional interest, and an increasingly diverse client base.

The event highlighted how strong brokers remain central to successful transactions, trusted guidance, and long-term confidence in Dubai property investment.

A platform for excellence

The 2026 awards built on the legacy of previous years by celebrating the professionals who maintain the highest standards of market knowledge, negotiation, and client care. They also reinforced Lapis Properties’ focus on developing a disciplined, innovative, and service-driven brokerage culture.

As Dubai continues to evolve, Lapis Properties remains committed to recognising the people who help shape its next chapter of growth.
